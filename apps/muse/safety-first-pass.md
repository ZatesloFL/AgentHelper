# Guided Flow: Safety First-Pass

**App ID**: `safety-first-pass`
**Title**: Safety First-Pass Audit
**Estimated Time**: 5 minutes
**Goal**: Set up Muse with the least access that still works, establishing safe boundaries before doing real work.

## Steps
1. **Understand Intended Use**: Clarify the specific task or role you want Muse to take on, and your comfort level starting out.
   - *Coach Question*: "What specific task or role are you hoping Muse will help you with first, and how comfortable or hesitant do you feel about it right now?"
2. **Review Default Connections**: Inspect the Connected Apps view to check which accounts (such as Facebook or Instagram) may be linked by default.
   - *Coach Question*: "Could you check your Connected Apps page to see what's linked by default? If you'd like, click 'Share My Screen' and we can look at it together."
3. **Decide Scope for Each App**: For every connected service, choose whether to keep it active, narrow it to read-only, or disconnect it completely.
   - *Coach Question*: "Looking at each connected service, would you prefer to disconnect it, limit it to read-only (like reading email without sending), or keep it?"
4. **Enforce Confirmation Gates**: Ensure sensitive actions—especially sending emails, posting messages, and purchases—strictly require your sign-off.
   - *Coach Question*: "Let's verify your confirmation settings. Are write actions like sending messages, publishing posts, or buying things set to require your explicit sign-off every time?"
5. **Audit Standing Rules**: Inspect saved permissions to verify that no unvetted "allow always" exceptions were granted.
   - *Coach Question*: "Have any standing 'allow always' rules been created? Let's check together so nothing can happen in the background without your knowledge."
6. **Model Training Opt-Out**: Review your privacy preferences and enable the model training opt-out if desired.
   - *Coach Question*: "Would you like your prompts and interactions excluded from model training? If so, we can turn that privacy toggle on right now."
7. **Low-Stakes Sandbox Setup**: Establish a separate, low-stakes secondary test account or folder for your first week of exploring.
   - *Coach Question*: "To keep things completely safe while learning, do you have a secondary email or low-stakes workspace we can use for testing this week?"

## Completion
Conclude with a calm, one-paragraph summary of active safeguards, confirm the user feels at ease, and invoke `save_session_note`.
